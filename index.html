the game plan

A general itinerary that works in any city

Swap in your own local spots, but this flow — fuel up, get active, get pampered, get loud — is the tried-and-true bones of a great bachelorette day. Built for a Friday-into-Saturday weekend, easy to compress into one wild day too.

10 AM
Stop 01 · Fuel Up

Bottomless brunch

Kick things off with mimosas, a big group table, and a menu that can handle the whole crew. This is where the sash and veil come out and the day officially starts.

12 PM
Stop 02 · Get Active

Rage room or axe throwing

Hand the bride a bat, a stack of plates, or an axe. Rage rooms and axe-throwing lounges are the perfect chaotic-good energy release before the real party starts, and they photograph incredibly well.

2 PM
Stop 03 · Get On The Water

Boat charter or pontoon cruise

A few hours on the water with music, drinks, and a killer view is the group photo op of the whole trip. Book a charter with a bachelorette package if the city's on a coast, lake, or river.

5 PM
Stop 04 · Reset & Glam

Spa hour and outfit change

Back to the house or hotel for a quick reset — blowouts, touch-up makeup, and getting into the night-out outfits. Order appetizers in so nobody's running on empty.

7 PM
Stop 05 · Eat Well

Group dinner at a lively spot

Pick a restaurant with energy — good cocktails, shareable plates, and a vibe that eases the group into night-out mode without needing a reservation for six hours.

9 PM
Stop 06 · Bar Crawl

Themed bar crawl through downtown

Hit 3-4 bars with a loose theme (matching sashes, a scavenger-hunt game, or dares for the bride). Keep it walkable and pre-plan the route so nobody's stuck making decisions at midnight.

12 AM
Stop 07 · Dance It Out

Club or late-night lounge

End the night on the dance floor. Grab a table or VIP section if the budget allows — it keeps the group together and gives the bride a home base to return to.

2 AM
Stop 08 · Soak It Up

Late-night bites

Every good bachelorette night ends with pizza, tacos, or diner food on the walk home. Non-negotiable.

mix and match

Activities to build your own weekend

Every bachelorette crew is different. Pull from these categories to build a lineup that actually fits your group.

🍸

Clubs & nightlife

Dance floors, rooftop bars, and late-night lounges. Look for spots that take reservations for groups and offer bottle service if you want a dedicated table.

💥

Rage rooms

Smash plates, electronics, and furniture in a padded room with a baseball bat. A high-energy, laugh-out-loud activity that's great for photos and stress relief alike.

🍽️

Eateries & brunch spots

Group-friendly restaurants with big tables, shareable menus, and a lively atmosphere — the backbone of any bachelorette day.

⛵

Boat charters

Pontoon rentals, sunset cruises, or a full-day charter. Bring a cooler, a speaker, and sunscreen for the best photos of the whole trip.

🍺

Bar crawls

A planned route through 3-5 bars keeps the group moving and gives structure to the night without over-scheduling it.

💋

Spa & pampering

Blowouts, mani-pedis, or a couple hours poolside. A great way to slow the pace down mid-weekend before the next round of festivities.

🎤

Karaoke & game bars

Private karaoke rooms, arcade bars, and trivia nights are a low-key way to get the group laughing without an early bar tab.

📸

Photo ops

A rented balloon arch, a neon sign backdrop, or a scenic overlook — one dedicated photo moment gives everyone the group shot they'll actually post.

🃏

Games & dares

A bingo card of dares for the bride, a scavenger hunt, or a "Mr. and Mrs." trivia round keeps the group engaged between stops.

before you book anything

Planning tips for a smooth weekend

01

Set the budget early

Send a group budget range before booking anything so nobody's surprised by a bar tab or charter cost.

02

Book reservations ahead

Group tables, boat charters, and rage rooms fill up fast on weekends — lock these in a few weeks out.

03

Build in downtime

Back-to-back activities burn people out. Leave an hour of breathing room between big stops.

04

Plan the ride home

Line up a rideshare, shuttle, or designated driver before the night starts, not after last call.
