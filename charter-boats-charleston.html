Best Boat Charters For A Bachelorette

A few hours on the water is one of the best group photo ops of the whole weekend. Here's where to look for a charter, from big party boats to smaller private sails.

on the water

Where to charter a boat or yacht

Big group party boat

Charleston Party Cat

Lux Cat · Karaoke Grande · Cowgirl Beach Club

A fleet built for exactly this occasion. The Lux Cat brings a full sound system and lounge-boat energy, while Karaoke Grande fits up to 40 for a floating sing-along. Great pick if the group is large and wants the boat itself to be the party.

charlestonpartycat.com →
Sailing & swim charters

Charleston Sailing Charters

Kismet · Serendipity · Southern Charm

Known as one of the go-to bachelorette bookings on the water, with the yacht Kismet holding up to 23 guests and the catamaran Serendipity built for swimming and lounging at anchor. A solid option for groups who want sand-bar time built in.

charlestonsailingcharters.com →
Private & intimate sail

OM Sailing Charters

Private catamaran · up to 6 guests

A smaller, more private option built around a single catamaran and a US Coast Guard-licensed captain. A good fit for a tighter bridal party that wants a quieter harbor sail with time to anchor and swim.

omsailingcharters.com →
Pontoon & sunset cruise

Stoked Party Boats

Private pontoon · day & sunset cruises

A more laid-back, budget-friendly pontoon option for harbor cruising, dolphin watching, and sandbar stops. Easy to book for a shorter window if the boat is just one stop in a longer day.

stokedpartyboats.com →
Compare listings

GetMyBoat

Marketplace · captained or self-charter

If you'd rather browse a range of boats and captains side by side, GetMyBoat is a rental marketplace covering Charleston with options across group size, boat type, and budget.

getmyboat.com →
before you book

Quick charter tips

Book weekends early

Weekend charters, especially Saturdays, sell out weeks in advance during peak season.

Check the BYOB policy

Most Charleston charters are BYOB with cooler space provided — confirm ice and cup policies ahead of time.

Match the boat to the group

Smaller sailboats top out around 6 guests, while bigger party cats can hold 20-40 — book based on your headcount.
