Bachelorette Party Planners

If decorating, fridge-stocking, and itinerary-building isn't how the maid of honor wants to spend her week, a local planner can take the whole thing off your plate.

a theme worth stealing

Last Toast on the Coast

Last Toast on the Coast is one of the most requested bachelorette themes for a coastal city like Charleston — think blue and white stripes, nautical details, captain hats, and a relaxed, coastal-grandma-meets-champagne aesthetic. It's an easy theme to hand off to a planner: give them the "Last Toast on the Coast" mood board and they can build the decor, gift bags, and photo moments around it without you lifting a finger.
