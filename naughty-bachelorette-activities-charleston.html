Bachelorette Party Activities

Beyond the basics, here's a deeper list of things to do — from game shows and karaoke to comedy, theatre, standout lounges, and private entertainment options for the bride.

play & laugh

Games, shows & entertainment

🎰

Epic Game Show Experience

A live, hosted game show night built for groups — think Family Feud-style rounds with the bride tribe competing on stage. High energy, zero prep required, and always a crowd favorite.

🎤

Karaoke nights

Rent a private karaoke room or take over the mic at a local karaoke bar. Great for groups that want to be loud and ridiculous without needing a dance floor.

🎤

Comedy shows

A stand-up show or improv night is an easy way to keep the whole group laughing together, and most venues can seat a bachelorette party as one block.

🎭

Local theatres

Check what's running at the local theatre scene during your dates — a show adds an unexpectedly classy beat to the weekend before the group heads out for the night.

charleston favorites

Lounges & bars worth building the night around

★

On Air CHS Club

A high-energy spot for groups who want music, drinks, and room to move without committing to a full club night.

★

Little Palm

A laid-back lounge with island-inspired cocktails — a great early-night stop before the group picks up the pace.

★

Peacock Lounge

Stylish and photo-ready, with a cocktail list that makes it an easy pick for a dressed-up stop on the itinerary.

★

Haberdash

A downtown favorite for groups who want a lively bar scene with solid drinks and a fun, social crowd.

...and honestly, there are too many great bars in Charleston to list them all here — this is just where we'd start.
